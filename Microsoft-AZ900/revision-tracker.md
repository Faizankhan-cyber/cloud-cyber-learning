## Review rules

- Prioritize repeated errors, confusingly similar services, and objectives with weak explanations.
- A correct guess is not enough to mark an objective secure; explain why the answer is correct and why plausible alternatives are wrong.
- Use spaced reviews for topics that were previously missed.
- Re-check the [official AZ-900 study guide](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/credentials/certifications/resources/study-guides/az-900) when the exam blueprint changes.
